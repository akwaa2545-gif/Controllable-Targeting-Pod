# Observer 0.5.7 / TGP 0.1.17 live smoke checklist

Not yet run in the game. Use a backed-up offline mission with room to recover; keep existing configs.

1. Replace both DLLs once each with the game closed. Restart and confirm 0.5.7 / 0.1.17. Check Esc then F8 still opens the settings panel with the previous initialization correction.
2. Enable Observer, select a live missile, and enter fullscreen with Hold disabled. Confirm the persistent AUTO HOLD INACTIVE / AIRCRAFT NOT STABILIZED band is readable, includes “Exit fullscreen to fly,” and remains beyond the temporary-notice duration. It must not promise pilot control while viewing.
3. With automatic Hold enabled but unable to engage under its existing limits, enter missile fullscreen. Viewing should remain open, Hold must stay inactive, and the warning plus specific refusal reason must appear. Do not deliberately create dangerous flight conditions just to test this; a controlled offline setup is sufficient.
4. In safe supported flight, engage Hold and enter fullscreen. The inactive warning must disappear only while Hold is enabled, available and active. If Hold later releases, the warning must return and the camera may remain open. Confirm no stale entry throttle/custom setting is reapplied by the fallback.
5. Make deliberate pitch/roll/yaw/throttle or other recognized manual flight input. Fullscreen should still exit and return native control under its existing safety boundary. Repeat after Hold releases, after late Hold engagement, after reopening, and with Hold disabled/unavailable. A stationary absolute throttle must not cause a false immediate exit on reentry.
6. Exit using the configured fullscreen shortcut and follow any release-input prompt. Test focus/menu/aircraft changes and missile loss. These genuine context/input failures must still close the view and log a precise reason once. Notification errors must not become a camera ownership operation.
7. Check the warning, return-key/status header, Hold details and transient notices at normal and small window sizes. Lower-priority content must not cover the persistent warning or header. The live font/layout and installed mod set remain unvalidated here.
8. With Auto Hold enabled and a healthy supported aircraft above the existing entry clearance/envelope, offset the virtual stick, then open the maximized map and paired TGP fullscreen. Confirm the old mouse offset clears, eligible Hold becomes ACTIVE, and further mouse motion operates the view without steering the aircraft. Press keyboard pitch/roll: Hold cancels, steering responds, and map/TGP stay open. Release keys and confirm no stale mouse roll returns. Repeat in missile fullscreen: mouse steering is ignored and keyboard/controller takeover exits to flight view.
9. Repeat below the entry envelope in a controlled offline setup: Hold remains inactive, cached mouse steering clears, and keyboard input still works. Do not assume neutral inputs level an already-banked aircraft. With Auto Hold off, map/TGP native behavior is intentionally unchanged.
10. Test an enabled direct Mouse Roll/Pitch/Yaw mapping only in a safe offline setup: expect a precise unsupported-binding refusal/closure, not a claim that this mapping is supported. Restore your normal bindings afterward. Test startup/reconfiguration source-proof wait and recovery, and missing/changed owner identity. Source loss must not leave a permanently captured zero-axis view.
11. Keep complete LogOutput.log, screenshot and exact reproduction steps for any unexpected closure, missing warning, stuck input or steering problem. Include the measured safety value/threshold now printed in the reason.
